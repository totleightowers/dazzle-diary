# Third-party licences

Dazzle Diary has no dependencies, but it does embed two typefaces. Both are
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits embedding in an application
provided the copyright notice and licence travel with them. They are bundled in
the app at `app/fonts/`, shown in the app under **Settings → Open-source
licences**, and reproduced verbatim below.

## Karla

    Copyright 2019 The Karla Project Authors
    (https://github.com/googlefonts/karla)

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full text: [`app/fonts/karla-OFL.txt`](app/fonts/karla-OFL.txt)

## Newsreader

    Copyright 2020 The Newsreader Project Authors
    (http://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader)

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full text: [`app/fonts/newsreader-OFL.txt`](app/fonts/newsreader-OFL.txt)

Each font ships with its own copy of the licence exactly as its authors
distribute it, rather than one shared copy, so nothing depends on a judgement
that the two are interchangeable.
